Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

FitTrack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by FitTrack.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use our application. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication data via Firebase.
  • Health Data: Weight, body measurements, and BMI calculations.
  • Location Data: When you use the running tracking feature, we collect GPS location data to calculate distance, pace, and draw your route on the map. On Android, an active run can continue recording while the screen is locked using a foreground service. Tracking stops when you end the run. Checkpoints are kept on your device so interrupted sessions can be recovered.
  • Workout Data: Exercises, sets, reps, and weights lifted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Calculate statistics such as distance, pace, and calories burned.
  • Sync your data across devices.
  • Ensure the security and integrity of your account.

3. Data Storage and Security

Saved account and activity data is stored in MongoDB, with authentication handled by Google Firebase. The application also keeps account-specific caches and active-session checkpoints on your device. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data during transmission.

4. Your Data Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request an export of your data (available in the Settings menu).
  • Request the deletion of your account and all associated data (available in the Settings menu).

5. Managing Your Information

Open Settings while signed in to export your data or delete your account. Account deletion removes your profile, saved activities and weight entries, and clears the account’s cached data on the device where you request deletion. A failed deletion remains pending and is retried by the server.
