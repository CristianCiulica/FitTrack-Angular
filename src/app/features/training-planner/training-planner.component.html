YOUR PROGRESSION RULE

Reps first.
Then resistance.

  1. Choose a load that leaves the planned good reps in reserve.
  2. Build reps inside the range while keeping technique and range consistent.
  3. When every set reaches the top with the planned reserve, try the smallest available load increase. Return toward the lower end.

If you miss the lower end, reduce the load or take more rest. With bodyweight, adjust assistance or leverage instead.
